City of Alamogordo · signed 26 December 2024

Flock Safety Statement of Work (signed) & Quotation

The actual contract documents. We obtained these in June 2026 in response to an IPRA request. The numbers tell the same story the May 2026 budget hearing did, but in primary-source form:

  • ~$250,000/year on a 24-month term (Year 1 $246,150; Year 2 $215,850; grand total $499,826.25 with NMGRT).
  • ~55 Flock cameras across six camera types (Falcon, Solar Falcon LR, Dual Solar Falcon, LPR Video Fixed, Condor PTZ, Solar Condor Fixed).
  • FlockOS Advanced + Community Rollover Program: up to 50 locations / 400 feeds, integrating privately-owned (Unifi) cameras into the same network.
  • Purchased via Insight Public Sector on the OMNIA Partners Cobb County cooperative contract. The city did not run a competitive bid.

We host both where we have them so visitors comparing against the city's own pages can find the file they expect. See the City's official minutes archive (CivicClerk, filtered to Flock meetings) for the canonical versions.

IPRA records received (June 2026)

On 8 June 2026 we received the following documents from the City of Alamogordo's IPRA office in response to a public-records request. The full return is in documents/foia-2026-06-09/; the README in that folder is the custodian's annotated index.

  • Flock Safety Quotation 0228029725 — City of Alamogordo (signed)
    Signed 27 December 2024 by Stephanie Hernandez, Acting City Manager · Insight Public Sector (OMNIA Partners Cobb County contract #23-6692-03) · PDF · plain-text fallback
    Itemizes all 55 cameras, Year 1 ($246,150) / Year 2 ($215,850), $30,300 in professional services, 24-month term from 2024-12-27.
  • Flock Safety Statement of Work v2.0 — City of Alamogordo (signed)
    Effective 16 December 2024; signed 26 December 2024 by Stephanie Hernandez · PDF · plain-text fallback
    Docusign envelope 1D116C6A-5F15-4D72-B7B1-5DD1C83DDC34. Scope: FlockOS Advanced + Community Rollover Program (50 locations / 400 feeds), Camera Registry Website, custom ESRI map layers, map attachments.
  • Flock Safety Master Services Agreement — City of Alamogordo
    Filed with the IPRA return · PDF
    Master agreement that the SOW references for general terms (privacy, data-sharing, indemnification). Price lives in the Quotation, not in this document.
  • APEX Technologies Service Agreement — Long-Term Storage Facility (signed)
    Signed 15 September 2023; $5,617.46 · David Kunihiro, Chief of Police · PDF · plain-text fallback
    Pre-Flock baseline: an NVR for a city "Long-Term Storage Facility" — i7-11700K, 64GB RAM, RTX 3060 12GB, 34TB. The GPU is over-spec for dumb recording. Useful context: Alamogordo was already building out analytics-capable, camera-extensible surveillance infrastructure for APD before Flock arrived.
  • APEX Technologies SSA — Evidence Room Upgrades, contract 24-066 (signed & redacted)
    Signed 18 March 2024; $9,364.36 · David Kunihiro, Chief of Police · PDF
    8 IP cameras + 2 RFID card readers + 2 UL electric door strikes installed in APD's evidence rooms. Includes a redaction log with one entry whose legal basis is "no reason" — see the README in this folder.
  • Custodian's notes & plain-text fallbacks
    Annotated index, full text for the Flock documents, Acroform-failed scan pages, and the readme README
    Includes a small procedural finding (a redaction log entry with no legal basis cited) and an Acrobat Sign audit-trail irregularity on contract 24-066. Worth reading before sending a follow-up IPRA.

How we source documents

All documents on this page are either:

  • Public records obtained directly from the City of Alamogordo (e.g. via IPRA, public comment at meetings, or the city's own website).
  • Publicly available documents hosted by a government agency (e.g. nmlegis.gov).

We do not publish documents obtained through private channels, leaked information, or anything that would identify a private individual.
